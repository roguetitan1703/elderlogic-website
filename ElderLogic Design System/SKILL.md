## Non-negotiable for ElderLogic

ElderLogic never rates, scores or ranks a senior living home — it shows what the Arizona Department of
Health Services has published. Never add star ratings, score badges, letter grades, rankings, or
red/amber/green status treatments to a home. Published values go in IBM Plex Mono with a source note.

Mobile is the primary composition, not a breakpoint: phone screenshots are presented at full weight.
