What this is

The database

Every licensed senior living home in Arizona, around 2,600 of them, with full licensing, inspection, violation and enforcement history from AZDHS. Refreshed monthly. One click to the state's own file.

The concierge

A search returns every home in range. Our team screens the record, makes the calls, confirms who has a room and agrees a price. You get the homes that work, not the list.

The field

Shortlists and territory areas become routed days on a phone. Stops, home details, post-visit notes. Routes can be sent to a family to open in Google Maps.

It does not replace an EMR or a CRM. It handles placement and territory work.

Coverage

Your liaisons are working from memory.

Ask a liaison how many homes they know in Mesa and the answer is about twelve. There are around 2,600 licensed homes in Arizona. Everything else in the territory is invisible, including homes with open rooms and homes that have never been called.

Homes decide which hospice gets the call when a resident starts declining. A liaison can only build that relationship with homes they know exist.

The concierge

Two hundred homes in the radius. Five you can actually use.

The search is the easy half. Somebody still has to read the state record, make the calls, find out who has a room today, and agree what it costs. That is our team, not yours. Homes you have blacklisted drop out before any contact begins.

Intake and the visit

Everything a liaison needs happens on a phone.

One assessment creates the client record, the power-of-attorney record and the placement search. Only the critical fields are required, so a home can give a fast yes or no.

The visit form afterwards is short and optional throughout. A long form never gets filled in a car park. A home that fails a visit is blacklisted once and stays out of every future search for everyone in the organisation.
