What it is

A placement and outreach platform for hospice teams, built on the state's own record of every licensed home in Arizona.

Who does the work

Our team screens, calls and negotiates. The software routes the days and keeps the records.

Where it fits

Alongside your EMR and CRM, not in place of either. Works on a phone, including routes, home details and notes.

Coverage

Twelve remembered homes, or the whole territory.

Liaisons work from the homes they already know. Everything else in the territory is invisible, including homes with open rooms and homes nobody has called. Homes decide which hospice gets the call when a resident starts declining, so a liaison can only build that relationship with homes they know exist.

Select an area and every licensed home inside it comes back with contact details and history, ordered into a route. We contact those homes ahead of the visit with your placement pitch, so the rep arrives somewhere that already wants the meeting.

Patients referred from assisted living average around 150 lifetime days on hospice against around 87 from home, which is why coverage shows up in census.

The state record

We do not tell you a home is good. We show you what the state published.

Every home carries its full AZDHS history, not only its current status. It is refreshed monthly, and one click from anywhere in the workflow opens the official state file. Your team reads it and decides.
