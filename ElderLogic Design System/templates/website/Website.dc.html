Hospice placement and territory work · Arizona

Your liaisons know twelve homes in Mesa.

Arizona has around 2,600 licensed senior living homes. The rest of the territory is invisible: homes with open rooms, homes that would refer, homes nobody has ever called.

ElderLogic holds all of them, with the state's own licensing and inspection record on each one. Then a person on our team does the calling. That combination is the product.

What happens, in order

One placement, start to finish. No argument in this section.

  1. 01

    An assessment is filled in

    It creates the client record, the power-of-attorney record and the placement search in one pass. Only the critical fields are required.

  2. 02

    The search returns every licensed home in range

    Around 200 of them, each carrying its AZDHS licensing, inspection, violation and enforcement history. Homes your organisation has blacklisted are already gone.

  3. 03

    Our team screens them and makes the calls

    We read the state record, contact the homes, confirm who has a room for this patient, and agree the price.

  4. 04

    You receive a shortlist that will take the patient

    Five or so homes with a confirmed room and an agreed price.

  5. 05

    The shortlist becomes a routed day

    Pre-tour visits in order on a phone, re-optimised when the day changes, with a short form at each stop. The same route can be sent to the family and opens in Google Maps.

  6. 06

    The move-in is recorded

    Which client, which home, what price, what date. A home that failed its visit stays out of every future search.

The same map runs territory work: select an area and get every licensed home inside it, in a routed order. ElderLogic does not replace an EMR or a CRM. It handles placement and territory work.

Why territory pays

A referral from assisted living stays on service longer.

Patients referred from assisted living average around 150 lifetime days on hospice. Referrals from home average around 87. Coverage of the homes in your territory is the input to that number, and census is what an owner is measured on.

Before a visit, we contact the homes on the route with your placement pitch. The liaison arrives somewhere that already wants the meeting: a resident who might fit, and no placement agent fee for the home to pay.

Questions we get

Where does it work?

Arizona today. More states are planned.

Does it replace our EMR or CRM?

No. It handles placement and territory work and sits alongside what you already run.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, including routes, home details and post-visit notes. Most use is in the field.

Where does the home data come from?

The Arizona Department of Health Services. Licensing, inspection, violation and enforcement history, refreshed monthly.

Who makes the calls?

Our team. Screening, contact and price negotiation are done by a person, not automated.

Do you rate the homes?

No. The state publishes a record and we show it to you. Your team decides.
