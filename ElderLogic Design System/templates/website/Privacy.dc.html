Privacy

This page describes what ElderLogic collects from this website, and how patient information is handled inside the platform. It is a plain-language summary and is not a substitute for the terms in a signed agreement.

What this website collects

If you submit the contact form, we receive the name, company, email address, phone number and message you enter, and we use them only to reply to you and to arrange a demonstration.

If you book a demonstration, the scheduling is handled by a third-party scheduling service, which collects the details you give it in order to place the meeting on a calendar.

Patient and client information

Patient and client records live inside the platform, entered by the hospice organisation that holds the relationship with that patient. We treat that information as confidential and access it only to operate the service, including the screening and contact work carried out on a placement search.

When we contact a senior living home on behalf of a customer, we share only what a home needs in order to say yes or no to an enquiry. We do not sell customer or patient information, and we do not use it to market to anybody.

Home and facility data

Information about licensed senior living homes is public information published by the Arizona Department of Health Services, including licensing, inspection, violation and enforcement history. We reproduce it as published and link to the original state document. We do not rate, score or rank homes.

Location data

For customers who enable visit verification, the platform records check-in and check-out at a visit location for staff using it in the field. That data belongs to the customer organisation and is used for its own reporting.

Retention and access

Customer records are retained for as long as the organisation holds an account with us, and are deleted or returned on request after it ends. A customer may ask us at any time for a copy of the records held for their organisation, or ask us to correct them.

Contact

Questions about this page, or a request about information we hold, can be sent through the contact form. A postal address and a dedicated privacy address will be added here before launch.
