The action control for CTAs, form submits and nav actions; one `primary` per view.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg">Request a walkthrough</Button>
<Button variant="secondary">See pricing</Button>
<Button variant="onDarkGhost" as="a" href="#">Talk to us</Button>
```

Hover darkens (never lightens or lifts); press nudges 1px down. On phone widths the hero CTA is `full`.
