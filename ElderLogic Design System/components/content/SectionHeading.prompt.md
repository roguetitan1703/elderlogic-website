Opens a section with an eyebrow, a serif headline and one lead sentence.

```jsx
<SectionHeading
  eyebrow="Every client, one place"
  title="Everything your team needs. Nothing they don't."
  lead="Client information stays organised and accessible without the complexity of a CRM."
/>
```

Sizes `xl | lg | md`; `tone="onDark"` on navy sections. Left-aligned by default — centre only for a full-width closing CTA.
