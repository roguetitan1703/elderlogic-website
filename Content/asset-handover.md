## The most important thing on this page

Terrah sent a screenshot we have been calling `state view.png`. It is the map
with a home selected, showing a card with that home's details. **It is the most
convincing thing anyone has produced on this project**, and it is exactly what
the weakest section of the website needs.

**We cannot use it as it is.** Three problems, in order of seriousness.

### 1. It shows a status on a home, which breaks Terrah's own rule

The card reads **"Good Standing Status: Good To Place"**.

Terrah's instruction to us, from the start, was that no home ever carries a
score, rating, ranking or status anywhere on the website. Her words: *"I didn't
say they're nice or not nice. The state is saying what they are."* We have
followed that rule everywhere in the copy and the design.

"Good To Place" is a status, and it is ElderLogic's own judgement rather than
the state's.

**We need her to tell us which it is:**
- an internal operational flag that must never appear publicly, in which case
  we crop or blur it, or
- something she is happy to show, in which case the no-rating rule on the
  website needs restating, because right now the site says in plain words:
  *"No scores, no rankings. Your team reads the record and decides."*

We are not going to guess this one.

### 2. It shows a real home's private details

Name, street address, mobile number and email address of a real business:
Sun View Estates Home Care in Phoenix.

Publishing that on a marketing website is a decision for Terrah, not for us.
Options: get that home's permission, blur the identifying fields, or use a
different home.

### 3. It shows "Open in Zoho CRM"

The card has a link reading **Open in Zoho CRM**. Terrah has been clear from the
beginning about not giving away how the product is built. This tells any reader
what the platform is.

We would crop or remove it. Flagging it so she knows we spotted it rather than
publishing it by accident.
