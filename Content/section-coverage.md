here is invisible for the life of the site. Check additions against this, not
against taste.

## The sections

Eleven. Three carry no copy, so the reading load is eight blocks against the
nine on the page the client rejected. Full bleeds fall at 3, 6 and 9, which
gives the page a pulse rather than one volume from top to bottom.

|---|---|---|---|

Section 7 is the differentiator and gets a section of its own. An earlier draft
buried it as one visually distinct row inside section 5, which under-weighted
the one thing that separates this from a search tool.

Section 8 exists because it is the only proof the project owns, and because it
explains why section 7 works at all.

## Corrections this pass made

1. **PRODUCT.md is wrong to say there are no customer quotes.** The client's
   sales deck, slide 6, carries a real message from a home. Fix that line.
2. **Intake is not data entry.** Her deck frames the short assessment as what
   lets homes answer yes or no at a glance. It was cut on a bad reading and is
   back, as an entry in section 5 rather than a section.
3. **The site's job was lost.** "The website will get sent up the ladder."
   There is a signed-ready company whose champion already knows her. The page is
   a credibility instrument before a persuasion one, and her stated failure mode
   is that it looks homemade. This changes execution everywhere.

---

## Decided

- **The add-ons.** Visit verification goes into section 10 as a capability,
  never with a price. Outreach reporting stays off the page and belongs on the
  call.
- **Coordinates exist.** Section 3 is unblocked and its marks are real homes in
  real places. Nothing about how the coordinates were obtained ever appears.
