## 7. Questions

> **Do we have to contact the homes ourselves?**
> No. Your team receives homes that have already confirmed a room and a price, and can approach any home directly at any point.

> The assessment carries patient information, so it is held under the same controls as the client record and visible only to your organisation's users.

## Banned vocabulary — none present

*seamless · empower · streamline · robust · leverage · unlock · elevate · transform · at a glance · purpose-built · comprehensive · cutting-edge · solution*

Checked against the finished copy. The decks used *at a glance* four times and *purpose-built* once; neither survives here.

## Banned constructions — none present

"It's not just X, it's Y" · "X isn't about Y, it's about Z" · gerund-stacked headings · rule-of-three as the default rhythm · every heading sharing one grammatical shape.

## Heading shapes — deliberately varied

| Section | Shape |
|---|---|
| Hero | Two clauses, one full stop **(1 of 2)** |
| 2 | Noun phrase |
| 3 | Statement with a demonstrative |
| 4 | Fragment, comma-joined |
| 5 | Plain label |
| 6 | Two clauses, one full stop **(2 of 2)** — her own phrasing |
| 8 | Imperative |

The shape you flagged appears **twice on the page**, against five times in DS Extended alone. Both uses are earned: the hero needs the contrast, and the record line is the client's own sentence.

## Sentence rhythm

Every section carries one long sentence — 35 to 44 words — against short ones around it. Section 3's is 41 words, section 4's is 41, section 6's is 44. Without them the page reads as staccato, which is its own kind of tell.

## The two rules

**No recipe.** Nothing on this page says how the record is assembled, refreshed, deduplicated or validated. *Refreshed: Monthly* is a fact about the record's currency, not a description of how it is done.

**No narrating our labour.** No instance of *we read, we call, we contact, we screen, we confirm, we negotiate, we agree, we verify.* The only first-person verbs on the page are **we keep** (the record), **we open** (the map, on the call), and **we show** (the record). The work is visible in the funnel and in the outreach log, and nowhere in a sentence.
