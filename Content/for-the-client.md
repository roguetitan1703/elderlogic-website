# Your new website

Everything you own, where it lives, and the few things worth knowing.

2 October 2026

---

## It is live

**elderlogic.app**

Anyone who types `www.elderlogic.app`, or clicks an old link to a page on your
Wix site, lands in the right place automatically. Nothing you had before is
broken. You do not need to tell anyone the address has changed, because it has
not.

---

## What you own

Everything is in your name. Nothing depends on us continuing to be involved.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Your domain** | elderlogic.app, at GoDaddy. Registered to you, exactly as before. We never moved it. |
| **Your website hosting** | A Vercel account in your name. This is the $20 a month. |
| **Your form responses** | An Airtable account in your name. |
| **Your booking calendar** | Your own Google appointment schedule. |
| **Your visitor statistics** | A Google Analytics account in your name. |
| **Your email** | Untouched throughout. We never went near it. |

If you ever want to work with someone else, they get the logins and the site
moves with you. That was the point of setting it up this way.

---

## When someone contacts you

**Two ways in, and both reach you without you doing anything.**

Somebody filling in the form on the site: the message arrives by email at
**hello@elderlogic.app**, and a copy is kept in your Airtable. The email is
what you will notice; the Airtable is the record, so nothing is ever only in
one place.

Reply to the person directly at the address shown in the message. Replying to
the notification itself will not reach them, because it comes from Airtable
rather than from them.

Somebody booking a demo: it appears in your Google Calendar as it always would.
You will get the usual Google notification. The website does not sit in the
middle of that.

---

## Changing your booking hours

The **Book a demo** button on the site opens your own Google appointment
schedule. So your availability is wherever you already set it, in Google
Calendar, and whatever you change there shows on the website straight away.

Nobody has to touch the website to change when you are free.

---

## You can cancel Wix

The old site is no longer serving anything. Your domain, your email and your
new website have nothing to do with it.

**One thing to check first.** Have a look at what else was on that Wix
subscription before you cancel. If you ever set up a form, a mailbox, a booking
tool or anything else in there, it goes when the subscription goes. If it is
only the old website, cancel it with no hesitation.
