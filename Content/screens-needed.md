# PART A: what you can capture now

Four shots. Two of them need you to put data in first, or they will look empty.

## Rules for all four

- **Make up a client.** Something like "Robert Alvarez". Not a real patient, and
  not Buggs Bunny, which is what is in there now.
- **Put data in before you shoot.** A list with one row, or a blank form, proves
  nothing.
- **Crop out your name and profile picture** in the bottom left.
- **No browser bar.** Just the app.
- **Send the original file.** Do not resize it.

# PART B: for Terrah

We are not asking whether these exist. We are asking **how to produce them**:
what to set up, what to click, and whether it is easier for you to capture some
of these yourself.

Everything below is for the website. Nothing goes on it without your sign off.

## What would help most

**1. A demo account with realistic data in it.**
The account we are using has one client (Buggs Bunny) and one marketing visit.
Any list we photograph looks empty. If you can either seed a demo login with
eight or ten plausible clients and visits, or tell us it is fine to create them
ourselves, that solves most of this in one go.

**2. Walk us to the route screen.**
We have a phone screenshot of a routed day: numbered stops, and buttons along
the bottom for Navigate, Room Details, AZDHS and FamilyTour Form. It is a
great image and it is on the site now. We do not know how it was produced.
What do we need to do to get back to it? The copy we hold also has test data on
it, a red "57 d 3 h 43 min late" tag and a profile picture, so we need a clean
one before launch.

**3. Room Details and AZDHS.**
Those two buttons on the route screen look like the most convincing thing in
the product. How do we get each of them on screen so we can capture them?

**4. The placement side.**
The site describes a placement from assessment through to move in. We can
currently photograph the assessment and the client record. For the middle and
end of that flow, what should we be opening, and does anything need to be set
up first for it to show real results?

**5. Anything you would rather capture yourself.**
If some screens are quicker for you to grab than to explain, send them and we
will work around whatever you send.

## Two things we need you to decide

**Naming homes.** The strongest thing we could put on the site is a real home's
state record, with its licence and inspection history. Showing a named home's
violations on a public page could upset that home. Options: use a home with a
clean record, blank the name, or leave this out. Your call.

**Real client data.** We will only use made up clients. If you would rather we
used a specific set of demo names, tell us what to use.
