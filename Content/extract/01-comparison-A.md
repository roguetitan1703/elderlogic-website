# Design approach comparison A

Source: `Website options/Design approach comparison A.pdf`
Extraction only. No verdicts. Copy is verbatim.

**Deck's own idea:** the coverage gap leads. Opens on the liaison's memory as the problem, then answers it with the map. Product explanation is deferred to a flat spec table rather than prose. Heaviest use of reserved/empty slots of any deck.

---

## S1 — Hero

- **Heading:** Your liaisons are working from memory.
- **Body:** They can name twelve homes in Mesa. Arizona has around 2,600 licensed senior living homes. The rest of the territory has open rooms, and no one has called it.
- **CTA (primary):** Book a demo
- **CTA (secondary):** What ElderLogic is →
- **Badge / micro:** AZDHS licensing & enforcement file — refreshed monthly
- **Asset ask:** none stated at hero level; map appears immediately below
- **Topic:** coverage gap

## S2 — The map

- **Caption left:** Each mark is one licensed home in Arizona.
- **Caption right:** Twelve marks are the homes a liaison can name.
- **Source line:** Around 2,600 licensed homes — AZDHS, refreshed monthly
- **Asset ask:** full-bleed Arizona map, every licensed home as a mark, with twelve of them distinguished
- **Topic:** coverage gap, visualised
- **Note:** the twelve-vs-2,600 contrast is carried by the graphic, not by a sentence

## S3 — What ElderLogic is

- **Heading:** What ElderLogic is.
- **Body 1:** ElderLogic is a placement and outreach platform for hospice teams in Arizona. It runs on a database of every licensed senior living home in the state, refreshed monthly, carrying each home's licensing, inspection, violation and enforcement history as published by AZDHS.
- **Body 2:** Your team files one assessment. The search goes out the same day and returns every licensed home in the radius. Then our staff read the state record on each one, call them, confirm who has a room for that patient, and agree a price.
- **Body 3:** Your team receives the homes that will take the patient. The platform routes the visits, records what your rep saw, and keeps a home that failed out of every future search.
- **Spec table:**
  | | |
  |---|---|
  | Coverage | Arizona |
  | Homes in the database | ~2,600 |
  | Source | AZDHS |
  | Refreshed | monthly |
  | Record held | licensing, inspection, violation, enforcement |
  | Calls and pricing | done by our staff |
  | Works on | phone and desktop |
  | Replaces | no EMR, no CRM |
- **Footnote:** Sold as a subscription with a per-user licence. Pricing on a call.
- **Asset ask:** none — the table is the visual
- **Topic:** what it is / how it works / pricing signal

## S4 — Territory

- **Heading:** Every licensed home in the state is already on the map.
- **Body left:** A liaison covers the homes they remember. The map holds the ones they do not: the home two streets over with a room open this week, the home that would refer if anyone had ever walked in.
- **Body right:** Select an area and you get every licensed home inside it, with contact details and the full published record, ordered into a route for a single day.
- **Caption:** Phoenix metropolitan area. One pin, one licensed home.
- **Asset ask:** Phoenix metro map at pin density
- **Topic:** territory work

## S5 — The concierge funnel

- **Heading:** Two hundred homes in the radius. Five you can actually use.
- **Body:** The narrowing is the work, and people at ElderLogic do it. We read the record, we make the calls, we agree the price. Your team does not receive two hundred phone numbers.
- **Funnel data:**
  - 200 licensed homes in the radius
  - 60 contacted by our team
  - 20 replied
  - 5 with a confirmed room and an agreed price
  - 2 the family tours
- **Open question box (in-deck):** Open question for the client: are these figures typical, or are they one particular search? The page needs one line of provenance here before it ships.
- **Asset ask:** funnel graphic
- **Topic:** concierge value / price justification

## S6 — The routed day

- **Heading:** The shortlist becomes a routed day.
- **Body:** Stops in order, re-ordered when the day changes. At each one: navigation, the home's answers for this client, the state record, and a short form afterwards. A home rejected after a visit stays out of every future search for everyone in your organisation.
- **Asset ask:** none stated
- **Topic:** field workflow / blacklist

## S7 — Reserved slots

- **Slot A heading:** RESERVED — WALKTHROUGH VIDEO
- **Slot A body:** Six minutes of the product being used. Does not exist yet. This slot holds its place.
- **Slot B heading:** RESERVED — A CUSTOMER
- **Slot B body:** One named hospice, one sentence, one job title. No proof point has been supplied. The layout keeps the space.
- **Asset ask:** walkthrough video (6 min); one named customer + quote + job title
