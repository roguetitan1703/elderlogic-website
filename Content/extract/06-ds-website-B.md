# ElderLogic Design System Website B

Source: `Website options/ElderLogic Design System Website B.pdf`
Extraction only. No verdicts. Copy is verbatim.

**Deck's own idea:** an interactive stepper. The hero hands the reader a four-tab control — Assessment / Concierge calls / Pre-tour route / Move-in recorded — and invites them to step through a placement themselves. The only deck built around a component the reader operates rather than scrolls past. Shortest overall.

---

## S0 — Header

- **Nav:** Placement · Territory · State record · About
- **CTA:** Book a demo

## S1 — Hero

- **Eyebrow:** PLACEMENT & TERRITORY — ARIZONA
- **Heading:** Every licensed home in the state, and a person who calls them.
- **Body:** ElderLogic searches around 2,600 licensed senior living homes in Arizona. Our team screens the state record, makes the calls, and confirms who has a room at what price. Step through a placement below.
- **CTA (primary):** Book a demo
- **CTA (secondary):** Territory work
- **Stepper tabs:** 01 Assessment · 02 Concierge calls · 03 Pre-tour route · 04 Move-in recorded
- **Asset ask:** one screen per stepper tab (four)
- **Topic:** concierge + coverage, with the flow made operable
- **Note:** compresses the six-step flow to four. "Concierge calls" is the only place the word *concierge* appears in a step label in any deck.

## S2 — Step 01: the assessment

- **Eyebrow:** ONE PLACEMENT, STEP BY STEP
- **Heading:** One assessment, three records
- **Body:** The assessment creates the client record, the power-of-attorney record and the placement search in a single pass. Only the critical fields are required, so a home can give a fast yes or no.
- **Checklist:**
  - Client and power-of-attorney records created together
  - Critical fields only, so intake finishes in one sitting
  - Submitting it fires the search across every licensed home in range
- **Panel label:** ASSESSMENT
- **Asset ask:** assessment screen
- **Topic:** intake

## S3 — Three framing cards

- **WHAT IT IS** — A placement and outreach platform for hospice teams, built on the state's own record of every licensed home in Arizona.
- **WHO DOES THE WORK** — Our team screens, calls and negotiates. The software routes the days and keeps the records.
- **WHERE IT FITS** — Alongside your EMR and CRM, not in place of either. Works on a phone, including routes, home details and notes.
- **Asset ask:** none
- **Topic:** what it is / division of labour / fit
- **Note:** the shortest statement of the division of labour anywhere in the material — one sentence per side

## S4 — Coverage

- **Eyebrow:** COVERAGE
- **Heading:** Twelve remembered homes, or the whole territory.
- **Stat 1:** 12 — homes a liaison names in Mesa
- **Stat 2:** 2,600 — licensed homes in Arizona
- **Body 1:** Liaisons work from the homes they already know. Everything else in the territory is invisible, including homes with open rooms and homes nobody has called. Homes decide which hospice gets the call when a resident starts declining, so a liaison can only build that relationship with homes they know exist.
- **Body 2:** Select an area and every licensed home inside it comes back with contact details and history, ordered into a route. We contact those homes ahead of the visit with your placement pitch, so the rep arrives somewhere that already wants the meeting.
- **Body 3:** Patients referred from assisted living average around 150 lifetime days on hospice against around 87 from home, which is why coverage shows up in census.
- **Caption:** LICENSED HOMES, PHOENIX METRO
- **Source line:** AZDHS — REFRESHED MONTHLY — ~2,600 STATEWIDE AS OF AUG 2026
- **Asset ask:** Phoenix metro map
- **Topic:** coverage gap + territory + census, compressed into one section
- **Note:** the only deck that puts the 150/87 argument inside a body paragraph rather than in stat blocks

## S5 — The state record

- **Eyebrow:** THE STATE RECORD
- **Heading:** We do not tell you a home is good. We show you what the state published.
- **Body:** Every home carries its full AZDHS history, not only its current status. It is refreshed monthly, and one click from anywhere in the workflow opens the official state file. Your team reads it and decides.
- **Table:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Licensing | Full history |
| Inspections | Full history |
| Violations | Full history |
| Enforcement | Full history |
| Source document | One click to AZDHS |

- **Rule line:** No scores, no ratings, no rankings. A home your team rejects after a visit is blacklisted once and stays out of every future search for everyone in the organisation.
- **Asset ask:** none stated
- **Topic:** the record / no-rating rule / blacklist

## S6 — Close

- **Heading:** See it running on your own territory.
- **Body:** Twenty minutes. We open the map on the area your liaisons cover and you can see what is in it.
- **Reserved:** RESERVED — CALENDLY EMBED · Scheduling
- **CTA:** Book a demo
- **Asset ask:** Calendly embed
- **Topic:** demo offer, with a stated length
