# Client sales deck — Hospice Concierge Platform

Source: `Pdfs/Hospice Concierge Platform.pdf` (the client's own Canva deck)
Extraction only. No verdicts. Copy is verbatim.

**Why this file is here:** this is how *she* sells it. Every other file in this folder is how *we* have been writing it. The gap between the two is the useful thing — her deck leads with the customer's gain in the headline and puts the mechanism in small type underneath, and it never once describes her staff doing work. Read this one for tone, not for structure.

**Her headline pattern, throughout:** imperative verb aimed at the reader, then a benefit. *Capture What Matters Fast. Plan the Day. Work the Territory.* Never a description of the company.

## Slide 6 — Territory and concierge outreach

- **Heading:** Plan the Day. Work the Territory. Build the Relationships.
- **Sub-heading:** Give your team the freedom to plan their day. ElderLogic Concierge handles the outreach and routing.
- **Body:** Your team controls their schedule and territory. Reps choose when and where they want to market, and ElderLogic Concierge handles the outreach and route planning — so they arrive at communities already interested in meeting them.
- **Pull quote (styled as a message from a home):** …We do our own placements, saving you from paying any placement agent fees… Can't wait to meet!
- **Feature bullets:**
  - **REP-DRIVEN SCHEDULING** — Reps choose their own date, time and target area based on their territory and schedule.
  - **CONCIERGE OUTREACH & ROUTING** — ElderLogic contacts nearby communities with your placement value proposition and builds the route around homes interested in meeting your rep.
  - **WARMER INTRODUCTIONS** — Reps walk in with a reason to connect: your hospice can bring residents directly to the home without outside placement-agent fees.
- **Asset:** territory setup screenshot
- **Topic:** territory / warm introductions
- **Note:** the pull quote is the only piece of voiced proof in any source material — a home's own words, reacting to the pitch. It does the work a testimonial would do, from the other side of the transaction.
