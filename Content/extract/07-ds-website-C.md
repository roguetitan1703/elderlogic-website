# ElderLogic Design System Website C

Source: `Website options/ElderLogic Design System Website C.pdf`
Extraction only. No verdicts. Copy is verbatim.

**Deck's own idea:** the concierge leads outright, and the hero says it in a human sentence rather than a category description. A three-column block splits the whole product into *the database / the concierge / the field*, and the rest of the page expands two of those three. The only deck that resolves the funnel-provenance question instead of leaving a box.

---

## S0 — Header

- **Nav:** Platform · Coverage · About
- **CTA:** Book a demo

## S1 — Hero

- **Eyebrow:** HOSPICE PLACEMENT & TERRITORY — ARIZONA
- **Heading:** A person calls every home, confirms the room, and agrees the price.
- **Body:** ElderLogic searches every licensed senior living home in Arizona. Our team screens them against the state record, calls them, and finds out who has space. Your liaisons receive a shortlist that will take the patient.
- **CTA (primary):** Book a demo
- **CTA (secondary):** What it does
- **Floating caption over the hero image:** The pre-tour route. Every stop opens that home's answers for this client and its official AZDHS file.
- **Asset ask:** hero product image — the pre-tour route, with an annotated caption over it
- **Topic:** concierge leads
- **Note:** the only hero in any deck whose subject is a person rather than the company or the software

## S2 — What this is

- **Eyebrow:** WHAT THIS IS
- **Three columns:**
  - **The database** — Every licensed senior living home in Arizona, around 2,600 of them, with full licensing, inspection, violation and enforcement history from AZDHS. Refreshed monthly. One click to the state's own file.
  - **The concierge** — A search returns every home in range. Our team screens the record, makes the calls, confirms who has a room and agrees a price. You get the homes that work, not the list.
  - **The field** — Shortlists and territory areas become routed days on a phone. Stops, home details, post-visit notes. Routes can be sent to a family to open in Google Maps.
- **Closing line:** It does not replace an EMR or a CRM. It handles placement and territory work.
- **Asset ask:** none
- **Topic:** the whole product in three parts
- **Note:** "You get the homes that work, not the list" is the tightest statement of the concierge value in the material

## S3 — Coverage

- **Eyebrow:** COVERAGE
- **Heading:** Your liaisons are working from memory.
- **Body 1:** Ask a liaison how many homes they know in Mesa and the answer is about twelve. There are around 2,600 licensed homes in Arizona. Everything else in the territory is invisible, including homes with open rooms and homes that have never been called.
- **Body 2:** Homes decide which hospice gets the call when a resident starts declining. A liaison can only build that relationship with homes they know exist.
- **Caption:** Every licensed home in the metro, each one carrying its state record. Select an area and it becomes a route for a day of visits.
- **Source line:** Licensed homes statewide, as of August 2026: ~2,600
- **Asset ask:** metro map with area selection
- **Topic:** coverage gap

## S4 — The concierge

- **Eyebrow:** THE CONCIERGE
- **Heading:** Two hundred homes in the radius. Five you can actually use.
- **Body:** The search is the easy half. Somebody still has to read the state record, make the calls, find out who has a room today, and agree what it costs. That is our team, not yours. Homes you have blacklisted drop out before any contact begins.
- **Funnel data:**
  - 200 homes in the radius
  - 60 contacted
  - 20 replied
  - 5 room and price confirmed
  - 2 toured
- **Provenance line:** Figures from a representative Phoenix-area search.
- **Asset ask:** funnel graphic
- **Topic:** concierge value
- **Note:** first deck to answer the provenance question rather than flag it — though "representative" is still an unverified claim

## S5 — Intake and the visit

- **Eyebrow:** INTAKE AND THE VISIT
- **Heading:** Everything a liaison needs happens on a phone.
- **Body 1:** One assessment creates the client record, the power-of-attorney record and the placement search. Only the critical fields are required, so a home can give a fast yes or no.
- **Body 2:** The visit form afterwards is short and optional throughout. A long form never gets filled in a car park. A home that fails a visit is blacklisted once and stays out of every future search for everyone in the organisation.
- **Image captions:** Assessment — one form, three records · Territory visits — pick a day and an area
- **Asset ask:** two phone screens — assessment, territory visit setup
- **Topic:** intake / field workflow / blacklist
- **Note:** merges intake and the visit into one section, which no other deck does

## S6 — Close

- **Heading:** See it against your own territory.
- **Body:** Twenty minutes. We open the map on the area your liaisons cover and you can see what is in it.

- **CTA:** Book a demo
- **Asset ask:** Calendly embed
