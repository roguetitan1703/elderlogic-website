# Design approach comparison — Extended

Source: `Website options/Design approach comparison Extended.pdf`
Extraction only. No verdicts. Copy is verbatim.

**Deck's own idea:** the long version. Answers every objection on the page rather than saving them for a call. Introduces four devices no other comparison deck uses: a who-does-the-work attribution system applied to every block, an objection handled by name, a four-step map zoom, and a full FAQ. It is also the only deck that changes the CTA verb — **Request a walkthrough**, not Book a demo.

---

## S1 — Hero

- **Nav CTA:** Request a walkthrough
- **Heading:** Cover the whole territory. We make the calls.
- **Body:** ElderLogic is placement and outreach software for hospice teams in Arizona. Our staff contact the homes, confirm a room and agree a price, so your reps spend the day in front of people instead of on the phone.
- **CTA (primary):** Request a walkthrough
- **CTA (secondary):** What it is
- **Asset ask:** none stated
- **Topic:** concierge leads

## S2 — The coverage gap

- **Heading:** A liaison works from the homes they can name.
- **Body:** There are usually about a dozen. Arizona has around 2,600 licensed senior living homes. Every one of them decides which hospice gets called when a resident starts to decline.
- **Caption:** Licensed senior living homes, Phoenix metro. AZDHS licensing file — around 2,600 statewide as of 1 Aug 2026.
- **Asset ask:** Phoenix metro map
- **Topic:** coverage gap
- **Note:** only deck to date-stamp the count (1 Aug 2026)

## S3 — The second problem

- **Heading:** The other half of the problem has a date on it.
- **Body:** A patient has a discharge date. The coordinator working the phones against it loses days finding a home that has a room, will take this patient, and will take this price.
- **Closing line:** One assessment goes out and the calling starts the same day.
- **Asset ask:** none stated
- **Topic:** urgency / the discharge clock
- **Note:** this problem appears in no other deck

## S4 — What ElderLogic is

- **Heading:** What ElderLogic is.
- **Body 1:** A company in Arizona. An app holding every licensed senior living home in the state, each one carrying the licensing, inspection, violation and enforcement history AZDHS has published about it. And staff who read that record, call the homes, confirm a room and agree a price.
- **Body 2:** It is sold to an organisation and used by a team. Reps share a territory, a home your organisation rules out stays out for everyone, and a manager can see what was done.
- **Asset ask:** none
- **Topic:** what it is / how it is bought

## S5 — Who does the work

- **Heading:** Who does the work
- **Body:** Every piece of work on this page is marked with who does it. Half of it is not done by your team.
- **Legend:** Your team · Our staff · The software · The state
- **Eight attributed cards:**

- **Asset ask:** none — the attribution colour system is the visual
- **Topic:** division of labour

## S6 — Territory and census

- **Heading:** Census is what an owner is measured on.
- **Attributed pair:**
  - *Your team* — A liaison picks a date, a time and an area. That is the whole set-up for a day in the field.
  - *Our staff* — Before the rep leaves, we call the homes in that area with your placement pitch. The route is built around the ones that want the meeting.
- **Body:** Every licensed home inside the area comes back with contact details, its full state record and a driving order. The rep arrives somewhere that already agreed to see them: a resident who might fit, and no placement agent fee for the home to pay.
- **Stat 1:** 150 — average lifetime days on service, referred from assisted living
- **Stat 2:** 87 — average lifetime days on service, referred from home
- **Note box (in-deck):** Source line needed before launch. These two figures are the most challengeable numbers on the page and need a named source and a date beside them.
- **Asset ask:** none stated
- **Topic:** territory work / the owner's number

## S7 — Objection handled

- **Heading:** "We already have relationships."
- **Body:** You have relationships with the homes your reps can name. Those are worth keeping and this does not touch them. The rest of the territory is the part nobody has called, and those homes are referring to somebody.
- **Asset ask:** none
- **Topic:** objection
- **Note:** only deck that names and answers an objection in the page body

## S8 — The record

- **Heading:** The record is the state's. We keep it current.
- **Body 1:** Every home carries its full AZDHS history, not only its current status. It is on the home before anyone drives anywhere, and one click opens the official state file from anywhere in the workflow.
- **Body 2:** Placing a patient into a home with open enforcement is real exposure. This is when a team sees it.
- **Body 3:** This is built and maintained work, not a feed. The file is structured, deduplicated and refreshed monthly, and every home's phone number and email address is validated. That is why a rep can act on the map instead of looking a number up.
- **Panel — ARIZONA DEPT. OF HEALTH SERVICES · Refreshed 1 Aug 2026:**

| Inspections | Full history |

| Homes held | c. 2,600 — 1 Aug 2026 |
| Contact details | Validated |
| Source document | One click to AZDHS |

- **Rule line:** ElderLogic does not rate, score or rank a home. It shows what the state published, and your team decides.
- **Asset ask:** none stated
- **Topic:** the record / credibility / liability

## S9 — The map, four zoom levels

- **Heading:** From the whole metro to one home's file.
- **Steps:**
  1. The metro. Every licensed home on screen.
  2. The East Valley. What a rep can drive in a day.
  3. A neighbourhood. Homes you can tell apart.
  4. One home. What the state published about it.
- **CAPTURE NEEDED (3):** The map at neighbourhood zoom: a handful of separate homes on named streets, one of them selected.
- **CAPTURE NEEDED (4):** Facility detail: name, address, validated phone and email, licence status and dates, inspection and violation history in date order, and the link out to the AZDHS file.
- **Asset ask:** four map states at increasing zoom, ending on one facility record
- **Topic:** scale, shown by zooming rather than stating

- **Sub-heading:** Six steps and a hand-off. Each one says who does it.

| # | Actor | Step | Body | Asset ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 02 | The software | The search | Every licensed home in range comes back against what this patient needs, each carrying its state record. - **Topic:** the full workflow
- **Note:** this is the only deck whose funnel stops at 5 — every other version carries a sixth row, "2 the family tours"

## S11 — Getting started

- **Heading:** What it costs you to start.
- **Attributed items:**
  - *Our staff* — We set the account up, load your territory and your existing homes, and apply anything your organisation already rules out.
  - *Our staff* — We train the liaisons. The rep-facing part is a form and a route, so this is short.
  - *Your team* — One person on your side to name the territory and say who the users are.
- **Needed-from-client box (in-deck):** Needed from the client: how long setup actually takes, what support looks like after go-live, and whether there is a contract length. These are the manager's real questions and the page cannot answer them yet.
- **Asset ask:** none
- **Topic:** onboarding effort

## S13 — Questions

- **Heading:** Questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Which states do you cover? | Arizona. Every licensed senior living home in the state. |
| Do we have to contact the homes ourselves? | No. Our staff do the calling for placement searches and for territory visits. Your team can contact a home directly at any point, and it is logged either way. |
| Where does the inspection data come from? | The Arizona Department of Health Services. Licensing, inspection, violation and enforcement history, published by the state. |
| How current is it? | Refreshed monthly, with the refresh date shown. Contact details are validated in the same pass. |
| Do you rate homes? | No. No scores, no rankings, no grades. We show the published record and your team reads it. |
| What if a home turns out to be unsuitable? | Your team rules it out once and it stays out of every future search for everyone in the organisation. |
| What if a home falls through after we have committed? | The search stays open. We go back to the homes that replied and to the ones we had not needed yet, and the shortlist is rebuilt the same day. |
| Is this more admin for my liaisons? | A liaison fills in a date, a time and an area, and fills a short form after each visit. The calling and the routing are not their work. |
| Can a manager see what the team actually did? | Yes. Completed and missed visits, activity by liaison, and move-ins recorded against clients. |
| Does it replace our EMR or CRM? | No. It handles placement and territory work and sits alongside what you already run. |
| Does it work on a phone? | Yes. Assessments, routes, home records and post-visit forms are all built for a phone, because that is where the work happens. |
| How is client health information handled? | The assessment carries patient information, so this needs a proper answer here. **Needed from the client:** how PHI is stored and who can see it, and whether a BAA is offered. |
| How long does setup take, and is there a contract length? | Ask on the call and we will give you the real numbers. **Needed from the client:** setup time, support model, contract length. |
| How is it priced? | Monthly, per organisation and per user. We go through the numbers on the walkthrough. |

- **Topic:** objection handling
- **Note:** 14 entries. Two carry unanswered client questions in place of an answer.

## S14 — Close

- **Heading:** Bring the territory you cover.
- **Body 1:** Forty minutes, on a call. You give us the area your liaisons work. We open the map on it and go through what is in it: how many licensed homes, what the state has published about them, and which ones nobody has contacted.
- **Body 2:** You leave with a picture of your own territory whether or not you buy anything. Bring the manager who would roll it out, if you can.
- **Attributed prep pair:**
  - *Our staff* — Pull your territory and its records before the call.
  - *Your team* — Bring the counties or the zip codes you cover. Nothing else.
- **CTA:** Request a walkthrough
- **Reserved:** RESERVED — SCHEDULING EMBED — The calendar loads in the page. A reader never leaves the site to book.
- **Secondary path:** Not ready to put a time in the diary? Send a message and we will answer it.
- **Form fields:** Name · Hospice organisation · Email · Message · **Send message**
- **Asset ask:** scheduling embed
- **Topic:** demo offer, with the length and the agenda stated
