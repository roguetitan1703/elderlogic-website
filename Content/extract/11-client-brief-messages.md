# Client brief — message thread, 14–20 July 2026

Source: WhatsApp between Terrah and Om.
Extraction only. Verbatim where quoted.

**Why this file matters:** the nine decks answer *what should the site say*. This thread answers *what is the site for*, and the answer is narrower and more useful than anything in the decks. It also sets every practical constraint on the build.

---

## 1. The actual job of the website

> "I have a company w 10 users ready to go whenever I get my act together. They know us but **the website will get sent up the ladder** and I don't want it to look like I made it myself in 2005."

This is the brief in one line. The site is not a lead generator for strangers. There is a signed-ready customer whose champion already knows her, and the page has to survive being forwarded to people who have never met her and who sign things.

Consequences:

- The reader who matters most is the one who arrives with no introduction and no context — the positioning document calls them *executive leadership*.
- The page is a credibility instrument before it is a persuasion instrument. Looking homemade is the failure mode she names, not saying the wrong thing.
- It gets opened on a phone first, because that is what a forwarded link gets opened on.
- Anything that reads as a dig at liaisons is fatal here — the person forwarding it upward may well *be* the liaison manager.

## 2. What she says is wrong with the current site

> "www.elderlogic.app — very homemade and things have evolved a ton since then."
> "I need it to go with the vibe of our logos and industry being eldercare but mostly software and data. You know the deal."
> "I haven't put any screenshots of the app or desktop version in there because it hasn't been ready yet."
> "Happy to fine tune verbiage also — I wrote all that a while ago and the software has evolved since then."
> "I also haven't mentioned your state data at all either which will need to be included."

Four stated gaps: homemade look, no product screenshots, stale copy, **state data entirely absent**.

**Positioning, agreed in the thread:**
> Om: "it currently reads as a route-planning tool, but your real edge is the state data. That's what tells an agent which homes are safe to place in, and no one else has it. I'd build around that."
> Terrah: "Well, there's several important features that can standalone or work together… there's several ways this is cool and unique (one of which is your data for sure)."

She agrees the data is an edge but resists it being *the* edge. Worth holding onto when the record section gets written.

## 3. The secret-sauce instinct is hers, not ours

> "IDK how detailed we'd need to get. **IDK what to give away the secret sauce** but there's several ways this is cool and unique."

She raised this unprompted, before any of our decks existed. The rule against publishing the mechanism is not us protecting her from herself — it is her stated concern, and every deck has been quietly breaking it.

Reinforced by her own metaphor for the working relationship:

> "If it were a house I'd show you the plumbing and you'd say Terrah only the flooring matters."
> Om: "Show me the plumbing, I'll point at the flooring."

And her closing line in recording 16: *"I tried not to go off about the wiring behind the walls."*

**She is the plumbing. We are the flooring.** Every raw thing she has given us — the walkthroughs, the blunt lines, the German shepherds — is plumbing handed over on purpose, for us to decide what shows.

## 4. Where the data actually comes from

> Om: "we're due for the next scrape around the 15th. Do you want me to run it?"
> Terrah: "Yes we can do it again (you should be invoicing me for that each month btw)."
> Om: "on the addresses, we already decided this one, we're leaving them how the state publishes (E, St, etc) so we don't break real street names."

**Delpat runs the monthly scrape.** That is what "refreshed monthly" means and it is verifiable, which is good. It is also precisely the sentence that can never appear on the site — this is the exact thing the client flagged as giving away the ingredients rather than letting people taste the dish.

*"The record is the state's. We keep it current."* is true, defensible, and says nothing about how.

Also settled: addresses stay in the state's own formatting.

## 5. Product state and roadmap

- The app was in QA with Emmanuel in mid-July; the area-selection lasso and the AZDHS panel have since been fixed.
- SSO / user sign-up flow was being built separately.
- A **demo video** was expected "within a week or so" — but she is explicit it is not meant to carry the page: *"not that it would just be a video on the website or anything."* Three of our decks reserve a large video slot. She does not want the video to be the argument.
- **Multi-state build plan exists and is parked** pending her mentor review. Supports the FAQ line "more states are planned" — but it is unfunded and unannounced, so it stays a plan, not a promise.
- **Next product is for placement agents** — the hospice product plus two more modules. Not this site. Worth knowing so nothing we build forecloses it.
- She is raising money: *"Raising money for this is scary so I think I'm dealing with some paralysis."* The site is also, quietly, an asset in that conversation.

## What this thread changes

1. **The audience narrows.** We are not writing for a cold market. We are writing for one signed-ready company's leadership chain, and for the next ones that look like it.
2. **The no-recipe rule is confirmed by the client.** It stops being our editorial preference.
3. **The video stops being a structural slot.** She does not want it carrying the page. Reserved video slots in comparisons A, C and DS Extended should be reconsidered, not just held.
4. **Imagery has a real budget** — 18 iStock credits — which constrains how many photographic sections are affordable, and argues for product screens and the map doing the work instead.
5. **The state data is confirmed as under-used** by the client herself, not just by us.
