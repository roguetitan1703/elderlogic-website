# Design approach comparison B

Source: `Website options/Design approach comparison B.pdf`
Extraction only. No verdicts. Copy is verbatim.

**Deck's own idea:** leads with the product in the field — a real Tuesday on a phone — before explaining anything. Then a numbered 01–06 flow with an attribution line under each step naming who does it. Territory and the census argument sit in the middle; the funnel comes late.

**Reconstruction note:** page 1 is a phone mock surrounded by annotation callouts, so the extracted reading order interleaves the callout labels with the heading. Grouped below as they read on the page.

---

## S1 — Hero (the day, on a phone)

- **Eyebrow / status line:** Tuesday — four stops — Mesa and Gilbert
- **Heading:** This is the whole day, in the order it happens.
- **CTA (primary):** Book a demo
- **CTA (secondary):** What ElderLogic is →
- **Annotation callouts around the phone:**
  - **STOPS IN ORDER** — The visits for this client, routed. Re-ordered when the day changes.
  - **ROOM DETAILS** — What this home answered for this client's needs.
  - **AZDHS RECORD** — The published state file on the home, one tap away.
  - **NAVIGATE** — One tap out to directions, then back to the stop.
  - **FAMILY TOUR FORM** — Optional fields only. A long form never gets filled in a car park.
- **Asset ask:** phone mock of the pre-tour route, with five callable UI regions
- **Topic:** the field workflow as the hero

## S2 — What ElderLogic is

- **Heading:** What ElderLogic is.
- **Body:** A placement and outreach platform for hospice teams in Arizona, running on a database of every licensed senior living home in the state. It is refreshed monthly and carries each home's licensing, inspection, violation and enforcement history as published by AZDHS. Part of the work is the software. The calling and the negotiating are done by our staff.
- **Asset ask:** none
- **Topic:** what it is

## S3 — The six steps

Numbered flow, each step carrying an attribution line naming who performs it.

| # | Step | Body | Attribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Assessment | One form creates the client record, the power of attorney record and the placement search. | your team |
| 02 | Search | Every licensed home inside the radius comes back, with its published state record. | the platform |
| 03 | Calls | We read the record on each home, call them, confirm a room and agree a price. | our staff |
| 04 | Shortlist | Your team receives the homes that will take this patient. | our staff |
| 05 | Pre-tour | The shortlist becomes a routed day on a phone, with a short form at each stop. | your rep |
| 06 | Family route | The same route goes to the family by text or email and opens in Google Maps. | the family |

- **Asset ask:** none — numerals and attribution labels carry it
- **Topic:** how it works, end to end

## S4 — Territory

- **Heading:** A rep picks a date and an area. We warm the homes before the visit.
- **Body:** Every licensed home in that area comes back with contact details and its published record, ordered into a route. Before the day, we call those homes with your own placement pitch: a resident who might fit, and no placement agent fee for the home to pay.
- **Payoff line:** Your rep walks into a meeting the home already wanted.
- **Asset ask:** none stated
- **Topic:** territory work / warm introductions

## S5 — Why territory pays

- **Stat 1:** 150 days — Average lifetime hospice days for a patient referred from assisted living.
- **Stat 2:** 87 days — Average lifetime hospice days for a patient referred from home.
- **Closing line:** Territory coverage is the input to census. Census is what an owner is measured on.
- **Asset ask:** none — paired stat blocks
- **Topic:** the owner's business case
- **Note:** no source cited on either figure in this deck

## S6 — The concierge

- **Heading:** We make the calls.
- **Body:** One assessment creates the client record, the power of attorney record and the placement search together. Only the critical fields are required, so the search goes out the same day. Then people at ElderLogic read the state record on each home in the radius, contact them, confirm who has a room for this patient and agree a price. Homes your team has already rejected never enter the list.
- **Funnel data:**
  - 200 licensed homes in the radius
  - 60 contacted by our team
  - 20 replied
  - 5 with a confirmed room and an agreed price
  - 2 the family tours
- **Open question box (in-deck):** Open question for the client: typical figures, or one particular search? A provenance line goes here before launch.
- **Reserved slot alongside:** RESERVED — THE SHORTLIST — The screen a customer opens to see the five homes, with the room and the agreed price on each. No screenshot exists. Nothing has been drawn in its place.
- **Asset ask:** shortlist screen (five homes, room + agreed price per row)
- **Topic:** concierge value

## S7 — The family route

- **Heading:** The same route can be sent to the family.
- **Body:** Text or email. It opens in Google Maps and they drive the tour in the order it was planned.
- **Open question box (in-deck):** Open question for the client: is the family route public, or internal only?
- **Asset ask:** none stated
- **Topic:** the family-facing surface
