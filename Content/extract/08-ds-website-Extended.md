## S2 — What happens, in order

- **Eyebrow:** PLAINLY
- **Heading:** What happens, in order
- **Sub-heading:** One placement, start to finish. No argument in this section.

| # | Step | Body |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | An assessment is filled in | It creates the client record, the power-of-attorney record and the placement search in one pass. Only the critical fields are required. |
| 02 | The search returns every licensed home in range | Around 200 of them, each carrying its AZDHS licensing, inspection, violation and enforcement history. Homes your organisation has blacklisted are already gone. |
| 03 | Our team screens them and makes the calls | We read the state record, contact the homes, confirm who has a room for this patient, and agree the price. |
| 04 | You receive a shortlist that will take the patient | Five or so homes with a confirmed room and an agreed price. |
| 05 | The shortlist becomes a routed day | Pre-tour visits in order on a phone, re-optimised when the day changes, with a short form at each stop. The same route can be sent to the family and opens in Google Maps. |
| 06 | The move-in is recorded | Which client, which home, what price, what date. A home that failed its visit stays out of every future search. |

- **Closing line:** The same map runs territory work: select an area and get every licensed home inside it, in a routed order. ElderLogic does not replace an EMR or a CRM. It handles placement and territory work.
- **Asset ask:** none
- **Topic:** how it works

## S5 — The territory

- **Heading:** This is the territory, not a sample of it.
- **Body:** Homes decide which hospice gets called when a resident starts declining. A liaison can only build that relationship with homes they know exist. Every home here carries contact details and its full state record, and any area drawn around them becomes a day of visits.
- **Caption:** Licensed homes, Phoenix metro
- **Source line:** Source: AZDHS — refreshed monthly — around 2,600 statewide as of August 2026
- **Asset ask:** Phoenix metro map
- **Topic:** territory

## S6 — Why territory pays

- **Eyebrow:** WHY TERRITORY PAYS
- **Heading:** A referral from assisted living stays on service longer.
- **Body:** Patients referred from assisted living average around 150 lifetime days on hospice. Referrals from home average around 87. Coverage of the homes in your territory is the input to that number, and census is what an owner is measured on.
- **Stat 1:** 150 — average lifetime days — referred from assisted living
- **Stat 2:** 87 — average lifetime days — referred from home
- **Warm-introduction paragraph:** Before a visit, we contact the homes on the route with your placement pitch. The liaison arrives somewhere that already wants the meeting: a resident who might fit, and no placement agent fee for the home to pay.
- **Asset ask:** none stated
- **Topic:** the owner's number / warm introductions

## S7 — Territory setup

- **Heading:** A day of visits is set up in one screen
- **Body:** A liaison picks the date, the time and the area. Every licensed home inside it comes back with contact details and history, ordered into a route they can drive.
- **Asset ask:** territory setup screen
- **Topic:** territory workflow

## S8 — In the field

- **Eyebrow:** IN THE FIELD
- **Heading:** The day arrives as a route, not a list of addresses.
- **Body:** Stops in order, re-optimised when the day changes. Everything a liaison needs at a stop is one tap away, and the form afterwards is short, because a long form never gets filled in a car park.
- **Chip row — AT EVERY STOP:** Navigate · Room details · AZDHS record · Family tour form
- **Expanded chip (Navigate):** The next stop opens in the phone's own maps app. The route re-optimises when a visit runs long or a home cancels.
- **Closing line:** A home your team rejects after a visit is blacklisted once and stays out of every future search for everyone in the organisation.
- **Asset ask:** phone route screen; one panel per chip
- **Topic:** field workflow / blacklist
- **Note:** the chip row is an interactive device — four labels, one expanded. The other three panels are not written.

## S9 — The state record

- **Eyebrow:** THE STATE RECORD
- **Heading:** We do not tell you a home is good. We show you what the state published.
- **Body:** Every home carries its full AZDHS history, not only its current status. It is refreshed monthly, and one click from anywhere in the workflow opens the official state file. Your team reads it and decides.
- **Table:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Licensing | Full history |
| Inspections | Full history |
| Violations | Full history |
| Enforcement | Full history |
| Source document | One click to AZDHS |

- **Rule line:** No scores, no ratings, no rankings. ElderLogic surfaces the published record and nothing else.
- **Asset ask:** none stated
- **Topic:** the record / no-rating rule
