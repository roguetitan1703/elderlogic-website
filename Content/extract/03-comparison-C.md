Extraction only. No verdicts. Copy is verbatim.

**Deck's own idea:** the page as a document. A contents list up front, numbered chapters titled as the buyer's own questions, and product screens presented as numbered "plates" with captions. The most explicitly structured of the three comparisons.

**Reconstruction note:** the step table and the spec table are multi-column and the extracted reading order misaligns label to value. Both are reconstructed below against the identical tables in decks A and B.

## S3 — Chapter 02: Do I have this problem?

- **Heading:** Do I have this problem?
- **Sub-heading:** Ask a liaison how many homes they can name.
- **Stat 1:** 12 — Homes a liaison can usually name in a territory the size of Mesa.
- **Stat 2:** ~2,600 — Licensed senior living homes in Arizona, held with their published record. *(AZDHS — refreshed monthly)*
- **Body left:** Homes decide which hospice gets called when a resident starts declining. A liaison can only build that relationship with homes they know exist. The rest of the territory has open rooms, homes that would refer, and homes no one has ever walked into.
- **Body right:** Select an area on the map and every licensed home inside it comes back with contact details and its published record, ordered into a route for one day. Before the visit we call those homes with your placement pitch, so the rep arrives somewhere that already wants the meeting.
- **Closing line left:** A patient referred from assisted living averages around 150 lifetime days on hospice. From home, around 87.
- **Closing line right:** Territory coverage is the input to census, and census is what an owner is measured on.
- **Asset ask:** none stated
- **Topic:** coverage gap + census argument, merged into one chapter

## S4 — Chapter 03: Who does the calling, and what do we get back?

- **Heading:** Who does the calling, and what do we get back?
- **Sub-heading:** This is the part that is not software.
- **Section heading:** Two hundred homes in the radius. Five you can actually use.
- **Funnel data (with per-row attribution):**
  - 200 licensed homes in the radius — *search*
  - 60 contacted — *our staff*
  - 20 replied — *our staff*
  - 5 with a confirmed room and an agreed price — *your shortlist*
  - 2 the family tours — *your rep*
- **Open question box (in-deck):** Open question for the client: are these figures typical, or one particular search? A provenance line replaces this box before launch.
- **Three cards:**
  - **ONE ASSESSMENT** — Creates the client record, the power of attorney record and the placement search together. Only the critical fields are required, so the search goes out the same day.
  - **THE STATE RECORD** — Licensing, inspections, violations and enforcement as published by AZDHS, not only current status. One click opens the official file.
  - **BLACKLISTING** — A home rejected after a visit stays out of every future search, for everyone in your organisation.
- **Asset ask:** funnel graphic
- **Topic:** concierge value / state record / blacklist
