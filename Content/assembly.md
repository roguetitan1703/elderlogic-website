# Assembly — three full pictures

Three complete pages, built from the options bank. Each is a real position, not a variation. Read the trade-off at the end of each — that is where the actual choice is.

Shared by all three, not up for debate: no recipe, no narrating our own labour, one CTA verb, the branded icon set, the design system.

# The decisions underneath all three

These do not change with the route. They need answering either way.

**1. The funnel.** Five rows as published, six with *40 blacklisted before contact*, or three (200 → 5 → 2). The six-row version makes the customer's own accumulated knowledge part of the graphic, which no competitor can copy. It is also the version she gave first, and it is unsourced twice over.

**2. The story.** Her German shepherds. On the page as a quote in her voice, rewritten as third-person copy, or off. It justifies the visit, the form and the blacklist in one move and nothing invented will match it. It is also blunt, and the page is going to executives.

**3. The proof.** The home's message — *"We do our own placements, saving you from paying any placement agent fees. Can't wait to meet!"* Route 2 makes it evidence in section 2; Routes 1 and 3 make it a proof block near the close. Either way it fills a slot three decks have been holding empty.

**4. The number.** 2,621 (hers), ~2,600 (our decks), ~2,700 (positioning doc). One of them, with a date beside it.

**5. Video.** She does not want it carrying the page. Recommend: no reserved slot. If it arrives, it goes on the close, small.

**6. PHI and setup.** Both unanswered, both are executive questions, and this page is being forwarded to executives. They need answers from her before the FAQ can ship.

# What I would do

**Route 2's opening, Route 3's middle.** Open on the homes choosing, prove it immediately with the home's own message, show the territory — then let the walkthrough carry the centre of the page and let placement arrive inside it rather than as a separate argument.

That fixes the two things you named. The page becomes about them, because it opens on their market rather than on their blind spot. And it has one thing in it worth remembering, because the middle moves.

It costs the most in production and it depends on screens you have said you will supply. If those are more than a few weeks out, Route 1 is the honest choice and it is not a bad page.

Say which, and I will write it.
